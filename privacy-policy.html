Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

At Aroosh Online Tutors, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal data to provide our tutoring services:

  • Name – to identify you on the platform
  • Email address – for authentication, communication, and account recovery
  • Profile photo – to personalize your profile (optional)
  • Grade / Education level – to match students with appropriate tutors
  • Subjects – to enable accurate tutor-student matching
  • Learning goals or bio – to help tutors understand your needs
  • Qualifications & experience – for tutor profiles and verification
  • Availability schedule – to manage tutoring sessions
  • Payment information – processed securely by our payment provider; we do not store full card details
  • Device & usage data – IP address, browser type, pages visited, and session activity for analytics and security

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match students with qualified tutors based on subjects, grade level, and goals
  • To enable messaging, scheduling, and session management
  • To process payments and handle refunds
  • To improve our platform features and user experience
  • To send important service notifications and updates
  • To comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes

3. Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data:

  • Supabase – stores user authentication data, profile information, and platform data. Supabase provides secure cloud storage with encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Cloudinary – stores profile photos securely. Photos are uploaded via signed URLs and served over HTTPS.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With payment processors to complete transactions
  • With cloud storage providers (Supabase, Cloudinary) for infrastructure
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • With your explicit consent for specific integrations or services

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it for a longer period (such as payment records).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@arooshtutors.com.

7. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember your preferences (such as dark mode)
  • Analyze platform usage via Google Analytics to improve performance and features

You can manage or decline cookies using the consent banner shown on your first visit. Declining non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use the core platform.

8. Children's Privacy

Aroosh Online Tutors is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete that data immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@arooshtutors.com
  • Website: arooshtutors.com

CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you specific rights regarding your personal data. This section applies only to California residents.

Right to Know

You have the right to request that we disclose what personal data we have collected about you in the past 12 months, including:

  • The categories of personal data collected
  • The sources from which the data was collected
  • The business or commercial purpose for collecting the data
  • The categories of third parties with whom we share the data
  • The specific pieces of personal data we have collected about you

Right to Delete

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain exceptions required by law (such as completing a transaction, complying with legal obligations, or detecting security incidents).

Right to Opt Out of Data Selling

Aroosh Online Tutors does not sell personal data. We do not disclose personal data to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration. Therefore, no opt-out mechanism is necessary.

Right to Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights. This means we will not deny you services, charge you different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised your privacy rights.

How to Submit a Data Request

California residents may submit a verifiable consumer request by emailing us at privacy@arooshtutors.com with the subject line "CCPA Request." Please include sufficient information to verify your identity, such as your registered email address and full name. We will respond within 45 days of receiving your request.

Authorized Agents

If you designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf, we may require proof of your written permission and verification of your identity directly with you.
